Jargon Buster Glossary

Emergency Shield (Buffer)

Money set aside specifically for sudden emergency costs so you don't need to borrow money or feel stressed.

Liquidity

How fast and easily you can turn your savings into spendable cash when you need it immediately.

Allocation

Dividing your monthly income into simple groups like Bills, Fun, and Future Shield Savings.

Micro-Milestone

A tiny, super easy savings goal (like $15 a week) that gives you a quick feeling of accomplishment!

Yield

The little extra bonus interest money your bank pays you just for keeping your money saved there.
